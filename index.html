About Our Company

The Last Bench Story

We started Last Bench because we were tired of seeing Indian businesses get ripped off by overpriced agencies who promised the world and delivered a WordPress theme with a three-month delay.

Based in Erode, Tamil Nadu with a presence in Bangalore — we are a full stack development company, mobile app development company, and UI/UX design agency rolled into one tight-knit team. We work with Indian startups & SMEs and international clients who want quality work at honest prices.

The name? At school, the back-benchers were always the ones who actually figured things out on their own. We're those people.

What We Think

Principles We Build By

Not slogans. Not mission statements no one reads. These are the real beliefs that shape every line of code we write and every decision we make.

01

Good software should be boring to maintain.

Clever code impresses nobody when it breaks at 2am. We write clear, documented, boring code that anyone can pick up and run with. Maintenance costs are real — we design for them from day one.

Engineering
02

Indian businesses deserve world-class software.

For too long, Indian SMEs got stuck with clunky, overpriced software. Every textile merchant in Surat, every restaurant in Chennai, every startup in Pune deserves a product they're proud of — at a price that makes sense.

Mission
03

Shipping beats perfecting.

A good product live today beats a perfect product next year. We push for fast, working releases and iterate from there. Speed is a feature. Momentum is an asset. Waiting is a liability.

Product
04

Transparency is non-negotiable.

We tell you what's hard, what will take longer, and what costs more — before you find out the hard way. Fixed-price quotes. No scope surprises. No ghost disappearing acts mid-project.

Integrity
05

The best stack is the one your team can maintain.

We don't chase hype. If Go is overkill for a landing page, we'll say so. If Python is a better call over Node, we'll say that too — even if it means less billable hours for us.

Technology
06

Small teams build better products.

Large agencies produce mediocre work by committee. We're small and focused — everyone who talks to you also builds for you. No account managers playing telephone. Direct access. Real accountability.
